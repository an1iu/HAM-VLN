# Adding HAM-VLN demo videos

1. Put an MP4 video in this folder, such as ham-vln-r2r.mp4.
2. Optionally add a poster image to static/images/.
3. Edit static/js/demo-config.js and add one entry to window.HAM_DEMOS with title, src, optional poster, and caption.
4. The page automatically reveals the Demo section and its navigation link when at least one valid video entry exists.

Keep the example template videos (banner_video.mp4, carousel1.mp4, etc.) out of HAM_DEMOS; they are not HAM-VLN footage.
